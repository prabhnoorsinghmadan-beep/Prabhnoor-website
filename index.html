About Me

Hey! I’m Prabhnoor (people call me by my nickname Noor). I was born in Delhi and started my journey as a student-athlete in Bangalore in 2021. I’m a passionate show jumper, MUN delegate, and a 10th grader at a CBSE school. When I’m not riding or debating, I love making music in my free time. I’m a huge fan of rock music, with bands like Led Zeppelin, AC/DC, and Arctic Monkeys topping my playlist. I also play the drums, which is one of my favorite ways to express creativity. I’ve been to 9 MUNs and have chaired 4 others, which has taught me leadership, public speaking, and teamwork. Apart from that, I’m fascinated by technology and robotics, and I love working on projects that challenge me to innovate and create. Looking ahead, I aspire to join the Navy MARCOS, which has been my dream since day one. I’m driven, disciplined, and passionate about everything I do, whether it’s sports, academics, or personal projects.
